## What I do

I simulate real-world attackers end to end — from external enumeration and
initial access through privilege escalation, lateral movement and reporting. I
work comfortably across Linux, Windows and cloud, and I care as much about a
clear, reproducible report as about the exploit itself.

- Full-scope penetration testing and adversary simulation
- Privilege escalation and Active Directory / cloud attack paths
- Clear, reproducible reporting (the reason OffSec awarded me a free OSCP)
